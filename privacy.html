YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy matters.

How Cashtraq handles information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: August 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Cashtraq (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services.

Who we are

Cashtraq is a cashback tracking and savings dashboard concept. We may use affiliate networks (such as Awin) in future to route you to retailers and track referrals.

Information we may collect

  • Account details you provide, such as your name and email when signing up.
  • Support messages you send us, for example by email.
  • Basic usage data, such as pages visited, to help improve the site.
  • Cookie preferences, to remember choices such as cookie consent and dark mode.

How we use your information

  • To provide and improve the service.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.
  • To remember your preferences, such as cookie consent and theme.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to help the site work properly and remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some cookies may be set by third parties if we use embedded tools or affiliate links.

Affiliate links and tracking

If we use affiliate partners, clicking a retailer link may involve tracking to attribute referrals. This may use cookies or similar technology set by the retailer or partner network. This helps us understand which referrals came from Cashtraq.

Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with service providers who help us run the site, such as hosting, and with affiliate or retailer partners if you click through their links.

Data retention

We keep information only for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. To request this, contact us using the details below.

Contact

If you have questions, contact support@cashtraq.co.uk .
